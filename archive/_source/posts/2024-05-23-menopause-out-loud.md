Halle Berry and many other names we are familiar with are serving as spokespersons to get the word out about a woman's experience during menopause and with that of our clinicians. She said, "The inevitable midlife event that all women go through must be destigmatized. She tells us [here](about:blank) that after an instance of painful intercourse, she visited her doctor and described it as "I feel like I have razor blades in my vagina. I ran to my gynecologist and said, 'Oh my God, what's happening?' It was terrible," she recalled. "**He said, 'You have the worst case of herpes I've ever seen.' I'm like, 'Herpes? I don't have Herpes!'" And she was right. **Halle's body, like many other women, responds to symptoms of perimenopause differently, including vaginal dryness, which can irritate, burning, and pain with intercourse.

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/621b05_c575e049a7e14d21b9eced77db69c772~mv2.png)

Also new about menopause, marijuana to improve menopause symptoms? I first read an article at Katie Couric Media. I started my search and found that according to a 2023 survey conducted by [Dahlgren et al. (2022)](https://journals.lww.com/menopausejournal/pages/articleviewer.aspx?year=2022&issue=09000&article=00006&type=Fulltext), women may be turning to cannabis to relieve the taxing symptoms of menopause. The analysis included 258 individuals; 131 were perimenopausal, and 127 were postmenopausal. Here's more on [this](https://www.nursingcenter.com/ncblog/october-2023/cannabis-to-treat-menopause-symptoms) and the KCM story [here](https://katiecouric.com/health/aging/can-weed-improve-menopause-symptoms/).
