Chicken Sausage, White Bean & Kale Soup

From the IAWH Healthy Kitchen  |  September 2026

One pot, six servings, and no added salt needed.

A simple one-pot fall soup. Chicken sausage does the seasoning, and beans and kale make it hearty. No-salt-added tomatoes and beans plus low-sodium broth keep it friendly to your blood pressure.

Serves6
MethodOne pot
SeasonFall

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
  • 12 ounces Italian-style chicken or turkey sausage, casings removed
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 can (15 oz) no-salt-added diced tomatoes
  • 6 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) no-salt-added cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 5 cups kale, stems removed, chopped

Steps

  1. Brown the sausage. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the sausage and brown it, breaking it into crumbles as it cooks.
  2. Cook the onion and garlic. Add the onion and cook until it softens. Stir in the garlic for the last minute.
  3. Simmer. Add the tomatoes with their juices, the broth, and the beans. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer. Mash a few beans against the pot to thicken the soup.
  4. Add the kale and serve. Stir in the kale and simmer until it is tender. Taste before adding any salt; you likely won't need it.

Notes

  • Choose a sausage with the least sodium you can find, and check the label, because brands vary widely.
  • Chicken or turkey sausage has far less saturated fat than pork.
  • For more fall flavor, add 2 cups of cubed butternut squash with the broth and simmer 10 minutes longer.
  • Black pepper and a squeeze of lemon at the end are nice but optional.
